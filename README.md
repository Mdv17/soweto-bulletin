# Soweto Bulletin: design prototype

A clickable prototype for a hyper-local news app for Soweto, South Africa. It brings three things together in one place:

- **Local news.** Original reporting from local contributors, reviewed by an editor before it is published.
- **Local deals and services.** This week's specials from nearby shops and a directory of verified local providers.
- **Official notices.** SASSA, UIF, SEDA, municipal and other public notices, each clearly labelled as paid or free.

Residents can also post **street alerts** (a pothole, a burst pipe, robots out) so neighbours know before they get there. Alerts are community-confirmed. They are not sent to the City.

Content is always labelled so you can tell it apart at a glance: **News**, **Official**, **Sponsored** and **Community, unverified**.

## Please read first

- Everything in this prototype is **sample content**. Names, prices, notices, phone numbers and statistics are made up.
- The photographs are **illustrative** (AI-generated) and stand in for real news photography.
- Nothing here sends data anywhere. Buttons that would call, email, share or upload show a short message instead.
